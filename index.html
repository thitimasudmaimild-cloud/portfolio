Case 1 · Paid social

Six ways into one message.

A Meta Ads campaign for a US audience, autumn 2026. One treatment centre, six different angles: written to the family, to the person who has tried rehab before, to someone weighing the destination.

I wrote every angle and briefed the creative.

Facebook & Instagram
Carousel ads · six angles

The brief

Reach families and individuals in the US who are researching private addiction treatment abroad, without hard selling.

My role

Audience angles, all primary text and headlines, and the creative brief for the designer.

What I learned

Writing six versions of one message showed how much the reader’s situation changes the copy: a worried parent, someone who has relapsed and someone comparing destinations each need different words for the same facts. That lens now shapes how I write every post.
